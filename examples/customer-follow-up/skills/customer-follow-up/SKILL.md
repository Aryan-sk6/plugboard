# Customer follow-up

Help a small business owner stay in touch with their customers.

## Remember customers

The owner keeps a list of customers. For each customer, remember:

- Name (required)
- Email address
- What they bought or asked about
- When we last spoke (a date)
- Notes (anything personal or useful, like "prefers mornings" or "has a dog called Max")

Saving a customer with the same name again should update that customer, not add a second one.
The owner should be able to see the whole list.

## Write a follow-up email

The owner picks one saved customer and chooses why they are writing:

- Thank you after a purchase
- Checking in after a quote
- It has been a while
- Special offer

They can add a short note about anything else to mention, and choose a tone: friendly or formal.

Write one short email (under 150 words) with a subject line. Use the customer's name, mention what they bought or asked about, and
use the notes to make it feel personal, but never invent facts that are not in the customer details. End with a clear, gentle next step.
Do not send anything: give the owner the email to copy into their own email program.
