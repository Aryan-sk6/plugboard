# Invoicing

Help a small business owner make invoices quickly.

## Make an invoice

Ask for:

- Your business name and contact details (one box is fine)
- Customer name (required)
- Customer address or email
- Invoice number (required, for example INV-0042)
- Invoice date and due date
- The items: a table with Description, Quantity and Unit price
- Tax rate in percent (0 if none)
- Currency (for example USD, EUR, GBP, INR)
- Payment instructions or notes

Produce the invoice as a downloadable plain-text file (.txt) named after the invoice number, laid out neatly so it can be printed
or attached to an email: business details, customer, invoice number and dates, a line for each item with its line total
(quantity × unit price), then Subtotal, Tax and Total. Work the money out carefully and round to 2 decimal places.
Do not invent items, prices or details that were not given.

Keep a record of every invoice made (customer, number, dates, total) so the owner can look back at them.

## See past invoices

The owner should be able to see the list of invoices they have made.
